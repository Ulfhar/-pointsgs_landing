2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected

  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Pseudonymous game account identifier
  • Country or region (inferred from your connection)
  • Gameplay data (in-game actions, progress, session length)
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Advertising identifier, where available and subject to the applicable consent choices and platform settings

2.2 Data from Third-Party SDKs

The Game uses the following third-party services:

Service Provider Purpose
Unity Analytics Unity Technologies, Inc. Gameplay behaviour, performance analysis
Google AdMob Google LLC Displaying in-game advertisements
Unity Cloud Save Unity Technologies, Inc. Storing game progress and settings
Tenjin SDK Tenjin, Inc. (US) Optional installation attribution and campaign measurement, including gameplay events, in-app purchase data and advertising impression data

Google LLC operates as an independent data controller for data collected through Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Games Services, and processes it under Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy. For Firebase-specific data practices, see: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

2.3 Anonymous Authentication

The Game uses a persistent pseudonymous Unity Gaming Services player ID to operate your game account and save your progress. The ID is also used as described above for crash reporting and may be linked to your Play Games account as described below.

2.4 Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the Game signs you in automatically to record and display achievements. Google receives your achievement progress together with your Play Games player ID; your gamer profile (display name and avatar) is managed by Google under its Privacy Policy.

To keep your progress tied to your account, we link your Play Games player ID to your anonymous game account in Unity Gaming Services: Unity Technologies, Inc. receives a one-time authorisation code and your Play Games player ID for this purpose.

You can decline sign-in, sign out, or delete your Play Games data at any time in the Google Play Games app. The Game works without Google Play Games sign-in. To remove the link between your Play Games account and your game account, contact info@points.gs.

2.5 Optional Ad Measurement

When you allow ad measurement, we use justtrack on Android and Tenjin to understand how players found the Game and measure how our advertising campaigns perform. For these purposes, the SDKs may process device and installation identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID when available, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device model, operating system and app version, and installation and app-open information. On Android, this may include Google Play install referrer and campaign information.

Tenjin also receives gameplay event data, in-app purchase information such as product, price, currency, transaction ID and purchase verification data, and information about AdMob impressions such as the ad unit and estimated revenue. We use these data to measure what happens after players install the Game and evaluate campaign performance.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game requires users to be 13 years of age or older. At first launch, users must confirm they meet this requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 and comply with applicable law, including GDPR and COPPA.

  • We use pseudonymous identifiers for game accounts and services. If you contact support, we use the contact details and information you provide to respond
  • If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will delete any associated data and restrict access to the Game
  • Where applicable law requires permission from a parent or legal guardian for optional ad measurement, that permission is required before enabling it
  • Parents or guardians who believe their child has accessed the Game or that their child's data has been collected should contact us at info@points.gs — we will promptly investigate and delete such data if confirmed

10. Ad Measurement and Other Privacy Choices

In versions of the Game that offer the optional ad measurement choice, it is off until you allow it. You can play with it off. If your version has an ad measurement switch in Settings, use it to change your choice at any time. If you need help withdrawing your consent, contact info@points.gs. Turning off ad measurement stops future measurement through justtrack and Tenjin. You can request deletion of previously collected personal data as described in section 9.

The Game and your device also offer the following privacy choices:

  • Android (ad personalisation): Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Firebase Analytics: for questions about Firebase Analytics or requests relating to its data, contact info@points.gs. Firebase Analytics advertising features (ad_storage, ad_personalization) are disabled in the Game.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: we collect crash reports to maintain game stability. Reports may include the pseudonymous identifiers described in section 2.2.
  • Or contact us directly at info@points.gs

11. Consent Records

The Game records acceptance of the Terms separately and may sync that record through Unity Cloud Save. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
