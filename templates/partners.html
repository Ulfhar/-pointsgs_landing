Behind Lumen Grove

Points Game Studio

POINTS.GS is an independent Ukrainian game studio creating atmospheric games and magical worlds. We combine hands-on development with a product mindset: build, listen, learn, and improve.

Lumen Grove’s creator is Bogdan Kagitin. The game brings together forest farming, decoration, storytelling, and seasonal activities in a cozy mobile world.
