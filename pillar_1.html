Overview

A Living Forest That Grows With You

Lumen Grove is a cozy fantasy game about bringing a forgotten forest back to life. Grow plants, help the creatures who live in the grove, and restore the Magic Oak through small acts of kindness.

Core features

  • Cozy farming in a magical forest grove
  • Everyday care that earns Light and helps the Magic Oak grow
  • Themed decorations that make the grove feel like your own
  • Forest residents to help through small acts of kindness
  • A forgotten forest to restore at your own pace
  • Dynamic weather

How the grove grows

  • Planting, harvesting, clearing areas, and helping residents earn Light
  • Offer that Light to the Magic Oak to help it grow and gain levels
  • Care for the grove and its residents as you restore the forest

Small acts of kindness, every day

  • Plant and grow plants
  • Harvest resources
  • Clear new parts of the grove
  • Help the forest residents
  • Gain Light through these everyday actions
  • Give Light to the Magic Oak to help it grow
  • Decorate the grove as you bring the forest back to life

Decoration & customization

Make the grove your own

Choose themed decorations to give your grove a mood and style of its own.

Examples

  • Mushroom-themed decor pack
  • Gothic-inspired decor pack
  • Celtic-inspired decor pack

Features

  • Interactive animations
  • Touch reactions
  • Dynamic particles and ambient effects

A grove shaped by your care

  • Plants to grow and resources to harvest
  • Themed decorations to express your style
  • A Magic Oak that grows through the Light you give it
  • Forest residents whose needs give your actions meaning
  • Dynamic weather that changes the forest's atmosphere
