01 / Overview

About the Game

Short Description

Lumen Grove is a cozy mobile game about restoring a forgotten magical forest. Grow plants, decorate your grove, help its inhabitants, and bring light back to a world touched by darkness.

Extended Description

In Lumen Grove, you are invited to bring an abandoned magical forest back to life.

Clear cursed stones, plant and nurture magical trees and flowers, gather resources, and decorate your own little corner of the forest. Every new plant and decoration helps transform the once-dark grove into a place full of life and colour.

At the heart of the forest stands the Great Oak. As you care for the grove and help its inhabitants, you collect Light — a magical energy that can be offered to the ancient tree to help it grow and unlock new possibilities.

Along the way, fulfil requests from the forest folk, discover fragments of the world's lore, complete daily tasks, and take part in seasonal events.

Lumen Grove is designed around the simple pleasure of watching something grow. It's a little magical world you can return to, care for, and make your own.

05 / A Living World

LiveOps & Seasonal Content

Lumen Grove continues to grow through seasonal events, themed decorations, new activities, and stories. The Halloween event and Witch's Cauldron mini-game bring this LiveOps approach to life, giving players fresh experiences and reasons to return to the grove.

Halloween & Witch's Cauldron

A Little Spooky, Never Scary

For its first Halloween event, Lumen Grove takes on a darker, more mysterious atmosphere.

The forest is filled with autumn colours, spooky decorations, and seasonal surprises. Players can discover themed content, explore new stories, and try a special Halloween mini-game: Witch's Cauldron.

Witch's Cauldron — A Magical Memory Challenge

Ever wondered what it's like to be a witch brewing mysterious potions?

In Witch's Cauldron, players briefly study a potion recipe, memorise its ingredients, and then try to recreate it by collecting the right items from a moving conveyor.

The recipes become more challenging as players progress, putting their memory and concentration to the test.

There are no ads or energy limits inside the mini-game, so players can enjoy the challenge at their own pace.

The mini-game becomes accessible after completing Lumen Grove's short introductory tutorial.

Halloween Event Highlights

  • A seasonal transformation of the magical forest
  • Halloween-themed decorations and atmosphere
  • Witch's Cauldron memory mini-game
  • Special activities and rewards
  • New fragments of forest lore

06 / Developer

Behind the Grove

About Points Game Studio

Points Game Studio is an independent Ukrainian game studio focused on creating atmospheric games, magical worlds, and memorable experiences.

Lumen Grove reflects the studio's passion for fantasy, storytelling, and creative game design. From its hand-crafted illustrations and magical atmosphere to its gameplay mechanics and rich world-building, every element is designed to bring a living, enchanting world to life.

The vision behind Lumen Grove is simple: to create a cozy fantasy world where players can relax, express their creativity, and enjoy the process of growing, building, and discovering something new at their own pace.

LiveOps keep this world growing after launch through seasonal events, new activities, and stories. Halloween and Witch's Cauldron are examples of this approach, bringing themed decorations, fresh atmosphere, and a magical memory challenge to the grove.

07 / World Building

World & Lore

A Little Light in a World of Darkness

Deep in the Far-Far-Forest, where the sun has never risen and darkness has ruled for centuries, something extraordinary has happened.

For the first time in a thousand years, a tiny Magic Oak has emerged from the thorn-covered earth, glowing with warm golden light.

The first to discover it was Nur, a cheerful little elf who never gave up on his forest. Even in the darkest times, he continued doing small acts of kindness every day. Not because he expected a miracle, but simply because he believed it was the right thing to do.

Now, the forest folk can hardly believe their eyes. The Gnomes are sceptical, the Fairies have their doubts, and many have long forgotten what the light of a Magic Oak even looks like.

But Nur believes. And he is determined to care for this fragile little tree, no matter what.

The darkness is still vast, and the Oak is only a tiny sprout. Yet, for the first time in a thousand years, hope has returned to the Far-Far-Forest.

Perhaps even the smallest good deeds can bring a little light into the darkest of times.
