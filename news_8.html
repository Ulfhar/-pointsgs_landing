Jackdaw Post: Bringing Forest News to Lumen Grove

A forest newspaper delivered by jackdaws — and a way for Lumen Grove to react to players’ progress.

Bogdan

Game Developer
October 15, 2026 ·3 min read

I’m working on the last feature planned at the start of Lumen Grove’s development — a cozy mobile game about restoring a magical forest, developed by points.gs. It’s called Jackdaw Post, a forest newspaper delivered by jackdaws.

From the start, I wanted a messaging system that felt like it belonged in the game. It gives me a way to communicate with players while making the forest feel alive: things happen, its inhabitants discuss changes, and news travels between the trees. The forest should feel like a place with its own life, rather than just scenery for another farming game.

Ideally, you’d read an issue without thinking, “Oh, this is a message from the developer.” It should sound like someone who actually lives in this forest wrote it.

That’s where the idea of a forest newspaper delivered by jackdaws came from. They have a monopoly on forest news. It’s a slightly funny, distinctive image that gives Lumen Grove another detail to be remembered by.

Why jackdaws?

Ravens were actually my first choice, much like in Game of Thrones. But they already have a different reputation in this forest: they steal acorns and fireflies.

Players can recover those rewards by watching an ad. That’s another example of how I approach these systems: I try to give them a place in the world, even when it comes to advertising. Watching an ad still takes you out of the game, of course, but at least the interaction around it has some context.

There’s also the story of the King of Ravens, which doesn’t exactly help their reputation. Giving them the newspaper felt like the wrong fit.

Pigeons? A bit too obvious for me. I also picture them as bright, gentle birds, which doesn’t quite match this dark forest. Jackdaws felt right.

What will be in the paper?

Thanks to Unity’s services, I’ll be able to publish news without releasing a game update through Google Play. That gives me room to add stories as I go.

There will also be articles prepared in advance, tied to progress in the grove. The Great Oak has grown stronger? That’s news. A large part of the grove has been cleared of cursed stones? The local paper should have something to say about it.

Through the newspaper, players can see the forest respond to their progress, read about its inhabitants, and collect extra rewards. It also gives me more room to develop the life and stories of Far Far Forest.

Sounds like a win-win.

Announcements about game updates and events already have their own place: pop-ups shown when the game starts. Jackdaw Post is there to cover life inside the forest.

Still in development

The feature is in active development. Testing will focus on how players use the newspaper: whether they open articles, how much time they spend reading, and whether they return. Analytics will help check whether the format works as intended.

I’ll keep this article updated as Jackdaw Post takes shape. Stay in touch!

Lumen Grove is available on Android through Google Play.
